## What TreeShift does

TreeShift maps forest conditions across the contiguous United States, under recent and projected future climates. It is based on data from the US Forest Service's Forest Inventory and Analysis (FIA) program, which surveys about 160,000 plots across the contiguous US.

Models are run in real time based on your selected settings. For any location, TreeShift looks at FIA plots that are nearby and have a similar climate, and summarizes what grows on them. It can map:

- where a species or forest type occurs, and how abundant it is;
- basal area, stem counts, and life stages, from seedlings to large trees;
- measures of the whole tree community such as species richness.

Under a future climate, it asks which nearby plots currently have the climate that a location is projected to have, and summarizes those instead. Comparing the two shows where conditions may become more or less favorable.

TreeShift is meant to support planning conversations: which species may be gaining or losing suitable climate in a landscape, where regeneration looks weak, and how connectivity may constrain range shifts as conditions shift. It is not a forecast of what will happen at any particular site.
